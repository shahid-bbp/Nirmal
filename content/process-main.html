THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE GOODNESS

Rice milling.
Handled with care.

Understanding how rice is processed begins with the milling stage. The outer husk is carefully removed while preserving the grain's nutritional value and quality as much as possible.

Hull
20%
Bran layers

The paddy first passes through an indent cylinder, where undersized grains are separated from whole grains. Controlled milling removes the husk and bran layers while minimising broken kernels. Whole grains continue for grading, polishing and quality inspection before packaging.

By-products such as husk, bran and broken rice are utilised efficiently in secondary applications. Our advanced process maximises yield while preserving the natural characteristics, texture and flavour of every grain.

A KERALA FAVOURITE, CAREFULLY PREPARED

Parboiled rice
processing.

Approximately 50% of our total production consists of parboiled rice, one of Kerala's most preferred rice varieties.

Three essential stages improve grain strength, enhance nutritional retention and give the rice its distinctive texture and cooking characteristics.

01Soaking+

The paddy is soaked before steaming. Water and processing conditions are carefully managed for consistency.

02Steaming+

Steaming is part of the controlled parboiling process, helping improve grain strength and nutritional retention before milling.

03Drying+

The steamed paddy is dried before milling. Each stage is monitored for product consistency and responsible water management.

01

Effluent
treatment plant

Wastewater generated from soaking during parboiled rice processing contains organic matter that requires specialised treatment before discharge.

Our plant, developed by Galaxy Environ ETP using technology licensed from CSIR-NIIST, applies biological anaerobic treatment followed by chemical treatment to meet environmental discharge standards and significantly reduce water pollution.

02

Water
treatment plant

Our advanced Water Treatment Plant purifies and recycles water used throughout the facility, reducing freshwater consumption while ensuring compliance with environmental regulations.

Recycled water is safely reintroduced into approved production processes, conserving natural resources without compromising quality.

03

Biogas
plant

Organic waste generated during rice milling is converted into renewable energy through our Biogas Plant.

Transforming organic matter into biogas reduces waste, lowers carbon emissions and generates clean energy to support our manufacturing operations.

04

Rainwater
harvesting

Our rainwater harvesting system captures and stores rainwater from facility rooftops, reducing dependence on external water sources while helping replenish groundwater reserves.

This initiative supports long-term water conservation and our commitment to environmentally responsible manufacturing.
